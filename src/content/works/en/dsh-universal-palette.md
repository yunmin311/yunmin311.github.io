## Project

Composer Search and a global Floating Palette share providers and deterministic ranking. Data comes from DSH public client APIs rather than a second catalog.

## Status and boundaries

Hero Composer Search requires the declared conversation.hero.composer.dock seat; on unsupported hosts it fails closed.
