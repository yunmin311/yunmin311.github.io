## Project

A canonical graph separates entities, relationships and evidence. The Web explorer reads that contract, with asynchronous analysis jobs and an evidence drawer. Shared content or history does not establish copying direction.

## Status and boundaries

Not a plagiarism detector. Similarity detection is reserved, not shipped.
