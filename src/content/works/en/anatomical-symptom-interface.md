## Project

The workflow keeps what the user said, a model proposal, a visual selection and a clinical finding distinct. It combines symptom descriptions, anatomical selection, interviews and structured pre-visit summaries.

## Status and boundaries

Clinical rules have not been reviewed by a qualified clinician; releaseReady is false. The 2D map is schematic, FMA bindings are unverified and anatomy coverage is limited. This is not medical advice or a diagnostic service.
